## What I made

Hime is a desktop companion who lives on the same computer as the person she talks to. She hears a microphone, answers in a voice rendered on that machine, and keeps a memory with real dates. Small talk is not written down. A faster model answers at once. A slower one continues when the question needs it.

She has a face. Emotion is a tag she has to say, mapped onto the avatar, not a guess from keywords. While she is quiet her head idles and she blinks. Her mouth follows the loudness of her own voice, so silence keeps it closed.

The part in progress is vision: a look at the screen that changes how she behaves, then a short note of what she saw. Those notes are not on this website.

## The constraint

On the desk she is private. Here she is a guide. She speaks prepared lines about the work. She does not watch your screen, and she does not remember you when you leave.
