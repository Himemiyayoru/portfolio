## What I made

Bob's Special Blend is a phone for cocktails that refuses to stay a list of recipes. The library holds more than 400 drinks. Search, favorites, and a cabinet sit beside it: mark what you own, and the app returns what you can make now and what you are one bottle away from.

Change an ingredient and the photograph of the drink gives way to a glass drawn from the numbers. Alcohol, sweetness, color, and whether the drink mixes or layers by density update as you pour. Substitutes are suggested by how close the ingredients are chemically, and the screen says what the swap did to the taste.

Bob is the bartender on the last tab. He runs locally, listens when you hold to talk, and recommends a drink. The recommendation can be wrong in the way a person is wrong. The glass cannot: its numbers come from the physics, not from his mood.

## The constraint

The character is the interface. The measurement stays outside the model.
